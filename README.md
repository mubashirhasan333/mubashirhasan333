# Mubashir Hasan


Mechanical engineer and PhD candidate focused on computational fluid dynamics, fluid systems, erosion/corrosion, asset integrity, CAD, and engineering data analysis.


I use simulation, experimental validation, and practical engineering judgment to understand flow behavior, equipment reliability, and design performance in mechanical and energy systems.


## Focus Areas


- Computational fluid dynamics: internal flow, multiphase flow, particle tracking, erosion prediction, pressure drop, heat transfer
- Flow systems and pipeline engineering: pressure-loss calculations, pumps, compressors, flow assurance fundamentals
- Asset integrity and reliability: FMECA, remaining life assessment, maintenance optimization, risk-based engineering support
- CAD and mechanical design: SolidWorks, Siemens NX training, SpaceClaim, experimental facility and test-section design
- Engineering automation: Python, pandas, NumPy, Excel/VBA, data cleaning, visualization, reporting


## Technical Stack


**Simulation and CAE:** ANSYS Fluent, ANSYS ICEM CFD, CFD-Post, SpaceClaim, SolidWorks Simulation  
**CAD:** SolidWorks, Siemens NX, SpaceClaim

**AI and machine learning coursework:** Machine Learning Specialization, Supervised Machine Learning, Advanced Learning Algorithms, Unsupervised Learning, Recommenders, Reinforcement Learning, AI Python for Beginners  
**Engineering domains:** fluid mechanics, heat transfer, erosion/corrosion, asset integrity, piping, mechanical systems
